Learning Alignment Mode

Constructive alignment revolves around making sure all components of your teaching cycle work together. Are your learning outcomes appropriate? Are you targeting the most important concepts? Are your teaching tools helping scaffold and consolidate?

Alignment pathways suggest ways to develop constructively aligned game-based learning options based on how spicy or creative you're feeling. Stick to the Safe Path (green) if you're just dipping your toes into the GBL waters or chart a course to the spiciest option with the Chaos Path (red) if you're feeling bold.
